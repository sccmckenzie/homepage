Supporting all stages of the data analytics value chain, I've encountered a recurring question: are we effectively harvesting value from our data? Far too often, most organizations hardly scratch the surface.

Overcoming this challenge has been the main focus of my career in advanced manufacturing. My journey began in the semiconductor cleanroom, using data to optimize the performance of our manufacturing equipment. At the time, our data was only accessible through homegrown thick clients, constraining our analysis capability and, subsequently, our impact. When our company rolled out a centralized data platform, we were suddenly able to **use our imagination**. This transformed my way of working and gave me a renewed sense of purpose which I carry to this day.

Now, AI has nearly eliminated the friction between our analytical imagination and execution. "Just add data".

In the post AI adoption world, the world 5 years into the future will be unrecognizable to us. Who knows if we still have dedicated data engineers, analysts, and scientists? I'm certain of one thing: data will always be important, and there will always be a need for folks who are passionate about unlocking hidden secrets embedded within data. My goal with this site is to share some of the organization & technical bits of wisdom I've accrued on my journey. Enjoy.
