About

Supporting all stages of the data analytics value chain, I've encountered a recurring question: are we effectively harvesting value from our data? Most organizations hardly scratch the surface.

Overcoming this challenge has been the main focus of my career in advanced manufacturing. My journey began in the semiconductor cleanroom, using data to optimize the performance of our manufacturing equipment. At the time, our data was only accessible through homegrown thick clients, constraining our analysis capability and, consequently, our impact. When our company rolled out a centralized data platform, we were suddenly able to use our imagination. This transformed my way of working and gave me a renewed sense of purpose which I carry to this day.

Now, AI has nearly eliminated the friction between our analytical imagination and execution. "Just add data!"

AI has also collapsed our horizon of predictability. In five years, will dedicated data engineers, analysts, and scientists still exist? One thing is certain — data will always matter, and so will people who are passionate about unlocking the secrets within it. This site is where I share the organizational and technical lessons I've accrued on my journey. Enjoy!
